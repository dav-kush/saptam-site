OUR STORY

WHO WE ARE

Saptam Infrastructure (SI) is one of the pioneers in EPCC (Engineering, Procurement, Construction & Commissioning) based in Vadodara, Gujarat. We have established ourselves as turnkey solution providers for Chemicals, Pharmaceuticals, Power, and Infrastructure Industries.

We deal with a wide range of services which includes design, engineering, material procurement, manufacturing, supply of plant and equipment, field services, construction, and project management in the fields of mechanical, piping, electrical, instrumentation, fabrication and erection — including heavy structures and equipment.

As an organization, we provide customization as required, offer guidelines for the latest economical processes and technologies for process improvement. Our approach toward construction management — streamlined, cost-effective, and state-of-the-art — has helped us deliver projects on time and within budget, while adhering to strict quality control and safety measures.

OUR SERVICES

End-to-End EPCC Engineering Solutions

From concept to commissioning, Saptam Infrastructure delivers precision-engineered solutions across 8 core disciplines — serving Chemical, Pharmaceutical, Power, and Infrastructure industries.

What We Deliver

As a full-scope EPCC company, we manage every phase of your project — Engineering, Procurement, Construction, and Commissioning — under one roof. Our multidisciplinary team ensures seamless coordination, strict quality control, and on-time delivery.

01

Design & Engineering

Our engineering team delivers FEED (Front-End Engineering Design) and detailed engineering services covering process, mechanical, piping, civil/structural, electrical, and instrumentation disciplines. We produce fully coordinated design packages — P&IDs, equipment layouts, isometrics, structural drawings, and 3D models — that form the backbone of every successful project.

KEY CAPABILITIES

  • Process design & simulation
  • FEED and detailed engineering
  • P&ID development
  • 3D plant modelling
  • Equipment sizing & selection
  • Hazard & operability studies (HAZOP)
02

Procurement

We manage the full procurement lifecycle — from vendor qualification and technical bid evaluation to expediting, inspection, and logistics. Our established supplier network ensures quality-compliant materials and equipment are delivered on schedule, reducing project risk and cost overruns.

KEY CAPABILITIES

  • Vendor qualification & management
  • Technical bid evaluation
  • Material requisitions & RFQs
  • Expediting & inspection
  • Import/export logistics
  • Inventory & warehouse management
03

Construction

Our construction teams execute civil, structural, mechanical, piping, electrical, and instrumentation works with a strong emphasis on safety and quality. We deploy experienced site supervisors, skilled tradespeople, and modern construction equipment to deliver complex installations on time and within budget.

KEY CAPABILITIES

  • Civil & structural construction
  • Mechanical equipment installation
  • Piping fabrication & erection
  • Electrical installation & cabling
  • Instrumentation & control systems
  • HSE management & compliance
04

Commissioning

We provide structured commissioning services that transition your plant from mechanical completion to full operational readiness. Our commissioning engineers develop detailed procedures, conduct loop checks, functional testing, and performance verification — ensuring your facility operates safely and at design capacity from day one.

KEY CAPABILITIES

  • Pre-commissioning planning
  • Loop checks & functional testing
  • Performance testing & verification
  • Punch list management
  • Operator training support
  • As-built documentation
05

Piping Fabrication & Erection

We specialise in the fabrication and erection of process piping, utility lines, and high-pressure systems. Our certified welders and piping engineers work to ASME, IBR, and client-specific standards, delivering leak-free, code-compliant piping systems for the most demanding process environments.

KEY CAPABILITIES

  • Carbon steel & alloy piping
  • Stainless steel & exotic alloys
  • High-pressure & high-temperature systems
  • IBR-compliant steam lines
  • Pipe stress analysis
  • NDT & hydro-testing
06

Structural Fabrication & Erection

From pipe racks and equipment platforms to large-span industrial buildings and storage structures, our structural team handles the full scope — design, fabrication, surface treatment, and erection. We work to IS, AISC, and client standards with full traceability of materials and welds.

KEY CAPABILITIES

  • Pipe racks & cable trays
  • Equipment support structures
  • Industrial buildings & sheds
  • Mezzanine floors & platforms
  • Storage tanks & vessels
  • Surface preparation & painting
07

Electrical & Instrumentation

Our E&I division delivers end-to-end electrical and instrumentation systems — from HT/LT switchgear, transformers, and power distribution to field instrumentation, control panels, DCS/PLC integration, and SCADA systems. We ensure safe, reliable, and code-compliant installations across all plant areas.

KEY CAPABILITIES

  • HT/LT power distribution
  • Transformer & switchgear installation
  • MCC & control panel erection
  • Field instrumentation installation
  • DCS/PLC/SCADA integration
  • Hazardous area (ATEX/IECEx) installations
08

Project Management

Our project management professionals apply proven methodologies — planning, scheduling, cost control, risk management, and stakeholder coordination — to keep your project on track. We use modern project management tools to provide real-time visibility into progress, resource utilisation, and risk status throughout the project lifecycle.

KEY CAPABILITIES

  • Project planning & scheduling (Primavera/MS Project)
  • Cost estimation & control
  • Risk identification & mitigation
  • Subcontractor management
  • Progress reporting & dashboards
  • Document control & management
